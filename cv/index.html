Professional Summary

Atmospheric physicist at the University of Bath investigating atmospheric gravity waves, middle-atmosphere dynamics and coupling through the atmosphere. Research combines satellite remote sensing, high-resolution numerical modelling and machine learning to study atmospheric variability and its representation in weather and climate models.
