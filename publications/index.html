Selected publications

These ten papers provide an overview of my research, particularly the development of new satellite methods for observing atmospheric gravity waves, their role in atmospheric dynamics, and their representation in numerical models. They are selected to give a flavour of my primary research area, as opposed to being (e.g.) my ten most cited or most recent.

2026

  1. ACP
    Stratospheric gravity waves in three high-resolution models and AIRS satellite observations
    Phoebe E. Noble, Haruka Okui, M. Joan Alexander, Manfred Ern, Neil P. Hindley, and 11 more authors
    Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics, 2026
    In three global models with 3–5 km grid spacing, 61–73% of detected gravity waves have amplitudes below 1 K, compared with only 10% in AIRS observations. Excluding these weak waves considerably improves agreement.
    DOI Bib
