I am <a href="https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/persons/corwin-wright/">Professor of Atmospheric Remote Sensing at the University of Bath</a>, where I lead research into the dynamics of the Earth's atmosphere, particularly atmospheric gravity waves and their role in weather, climate and the circulation of the middle atmosphere.

My research combines satellite observations, numerical modelling and data analysis to understand atmospheric processes across a wide range of spatial and temporal scales. I am particularly interested in developing new methods to extract information from satellite measurements, and in using these observations to improve our understanding of atmospheric dynamics and the performance of numerical models.

I have worked at Bath since 2013, initially as a postdoctoral researcher before taking up a <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_University_Research_Fellowship">Royal Society University Research Fellowship</a> in 2017. Before joining Bath, I held research positions at the <a href="https://ncar.ucar.edu/">National Center for Atmospheric Research</a> in the USA and the <a href="https://www.univ-brest.fr/fr">Université de Bretagne Occidentale</a> in France. Since 2017, I have led research projects supported by a range of national and international funders including <a href="https://aria.org.uk/">ARIA</a>, <a href="https://www.ukri.org/councils/epsrc/">EPSRC</a>, <a href="https://www.esa.int/">ESA</a>, the <a href="https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/">Leverhulme Trust</a>, <a href="https://www.ukri.org/councils/nerc/">NERC</a>, and the <a href="https://royalsociety.org/">Royal Society</a>.

Alongside my research, I co-lead the international <a href="https://aparc-climate.org/activities/gravity-waves/">APARC Gravity Waves Activity</a> and contribute to several international satellite missions (including <a href="https://atmos.uw.edu/strive/">NASA STRIVE</a> and <a href="https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/06/Keystone">ESA Keystone</a>) and atmospheric science programmes (including <a href="https://www.teamx-programme.org/">TeamX</a> and <a href="https://s-rip.github.io/">ARIP</a>). I also supervise doctoral and postdoctoral researchers, teach computing, atmospheric science and space science, and have previously served as Director of Research in Bath's Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering and as founding Co-Director of Bath Engineering's <a href="https://www.bath.ac.uk/research-centres/centre-for-climate-adaptation-environment-research/">Centre for Climate Adaptation and Environment Research</a>.
